Job Description – Affiliate Manager

About Us
We are a young and dynamic company with a strong presence in global markets. Our culture is based on teamwork, growth, and high performance. If you’re looking for an opportunity to make an impact, build strong partnerships, and grow in the affiliate marketing industry – this is the place for you.
 
Position Overview
As an Affiliate Manager, you will serve as the main point of contact for affiliate partners. Your focus will be on recruiting, supporting, and optimizing relationships with affiliates, ensuring their success directly contributes to the company’s growth. This is a hands-on role, not a managerial position, and is best suited for motivated individuals who enjoy both sales and relationship-building.
 
Key Responsibilities
•	Recruit New Affiliates: Proactively identify and onboard affiliates to expand the company’s marketing network.
•	Partner Relationship Management: Serve as the daily contact for affiliates, supporting them in campaigns, tools, and performance optimization.
•	Performance Monitoring: Track KPIs, analyze lead quality, and provide recommendations for improving results.
•	Negotiation & Agreements: Negotiate commercial terms (CPA, CPL, RevShare, hybrid models) and ensure win–win outcomes.
•	Growth Support: Help affiliates maximize their earnings while driving company revenue and market share.
 
Requirements
•	Experience in affiliate marketing, sales, or account management – Forex/Crypto industry is an advantage.
•	Strong sales and negotiation skills with a results-oriented mindset.
•	Ability to analyze performance data and draw actionable insights.
•	High proficiency in English – written and spoken. Additional languages (Russian, Spanish, etc.) are a strong advantage.
•	Energetic, dynamic, and proactive personality with the ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment.
•	Excellent interpersonal skills and the ability to build trust-based partnerships.
 
What We Offer
•	Competitive base salary plus performance bonuses.
•	Professional growth opportunities in a global company.
•	Dynamic and supportive team culture.
•	Hybrid model: Monday–Friday, with Fridays from home.
•	A fun, young work environment with excellent benefits.
